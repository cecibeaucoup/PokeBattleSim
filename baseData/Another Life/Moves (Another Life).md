Deals damage, then may make an opposed control roll against the opponent to initiate a grapple, with the roll modified by maneuver-based damage dice bonuses and penalties, with no modifiers from hits or weight. If the attacker wins, they initiate a grapple that lasts for the remainder of the round or until removed by a single attack dealing at least one quarter of their Wounds, applying all properties as per Bind save that the target's Evasion is not reduced for attacks by opponents other than the user. Immediately after successfully executing the a grapple, and each time the user hits the victim again with any other move while the grapple is ongoing, this move will attack again, applying onslaught penalties each time. If multiple users of this move successfully the same target, attacks by a partner will also trigger off added hits during the grapple. Contact.
- Teeter Dance (B): Can be used as part of either an attack or a defense. All opposing pokemon have their attack patterns altered to mirror the user's in matching order, though they are still free to choose their moves and targets.
- Alluring Voice (A): +8 hit dice, +10 damage dice, must be used as part of the user's first or only attack action. If a target that is hit by this move has used power-up moves during the same round prior to being hit, the numerical bonuses of these power-ups are doubled for the remainder of the round. Thereafter until the target switches out, these bonuses are reversed into penalties as a status effect, and continue to act as active power-ups of the same identities, preventing the target from stacking the same moves on top of them.
- Heart Swap (A): +6 hit dice. If this move hits an opponent, the user takes control of that opponent and vice versa for the remainder of the round. Both pokemon retain their movesets and actions in their new bodies, but can only use moves that their own species can learn. Any skills that either of them have that are higher than the other's will be reduced to match with an additional -1 penalty so long as control is swapped. Injuries and Energy expenditures accumulated in the other party's body remain with that body once the effect ends. Must target a single opponent, cannot affect a target twice until either the user or that target have been switched out and back in.
- Life Dew (A): While this move is active, the user or its ally are healed by 1 Wound for every time an Energy roll would be triggered by their moves against their species base Stamina. On the round during which it is used, moves by the user and ally that precede it (including Life Dew itself) will also grant healing in this manner. Lasts 2 rounds as a field effect.
- Moonblast (A): +4 hit dice, +12 damage dice. Ignores the Dodge bonuses from the Hover and Fly movement types, and benefits from double onslaught penalties. If this hits an enemy, removes all attribute and skill status penalties from the target, and grants their opposite to the user as power-up bonuses for their remaining duration, or for as long as both the target and the user remain in the match for those that do not expire, even if either is switched out. Can raise Skills above S-Rank. Accrued bonuses from multiple Moonblasts overlap. Can only attack a single opponent.
- Perish Song (A): -2 Priority. This move must be used as an attack. Once used, this move remains active until Priority 0 and may not be voluntarily stopped, but is interrupted for the remainder of the round if the user takes a number of Wounds from a single attack action greater than its Efficiency. While this move is active, the user may not use other sonic-based moves, and is immune to any other sources of this move. Allies and opponents exposed to this move for at least 8 Priority ticks incur a -1 penalty to all Skills, have all move costs increased by one grade after 12 Priority ticks, and all Abilities and trainer bonuses disabled at 16 Priority ticks. Any pokemon exposed to this for 20 Priority ticks is reduced to 0 Wounds. If the user takes 4 or more Wounds from a single hit, it stops building this move for the remainder of the round. This effect counts as a status, and builds exposure at an increased rate proportional to the number of users that a victim is being affected by. Any pokemon that is not exposed to this move on consecutive rounds loses all build-up.
- Wish (A): This move has no immediate effect, and at the time of use, the user must set an activation condition based on and Wound condition and optional recipient name. When the condition is met even if the user is switched or knocked out, the recipient is immediately healed of one Wound for every point of Power the user has, restoring them from knockout if applicable, and removing Wound-based attribute penalties at the highest applicable rate. If the condition is met on the round during which this move was used, it activates next round if still applicable. If the condition cannot be met, the stored move is canceled. Only one instance can be stored at a time per user, and can only affect a single ally.
- Wonder Room (A): Must be used as part of an attack action. The user chooses up to three types, and may apply the properties "always hit" and / or "always Wound" to them. Single targeting damage dealing moves or pokemon of a type designated to "always hit" will hit / be hit at 0 threshold even if they miss, but will allow the target to substitute their base Dodge (Speed + Evasion) for total Defense (not counting onslaught penalties) if this occurs. Damage dealing moves or pokemon of a type designated to "always Wound" will always inflict / take at least 1 Wound per rank as if Defense had been defeated by that much, applying secondary effects as appropriate, but do not break Protect, King's Shield, or Barrier. If this move is used again by any combatant while it is active, its remaining duration is canceled. Lasts 3 rounds, cannot be canceled during the first round.
- Light of Ruin (S): +8 hit dice, +20 damage dice, -2 Priority. If this move causes Wounds, it disables automatic successes from the target's same-type attack bonuses (but not from its Signature Move) for the remainder of the round, plus 1 round for every 5 Wounds inflicted. Each use of this move deducts 2 Energy Levels from the user at most. If it would otherwise have deducted more, each additional Energy Level that would have been lost will cause the user to take 5 Wounds instead. Automatically attacks both opponents in a double battle.
